# Game Design — Branching Tales

> Status: Draft scaffolding. Filled in next session.

## Concept

Branching Tales is a fantasy adventurer's guild simulator. The player runs a guild that recruits heroes, equips them, sends them on quests, and grows the roster as quests are completed. The demo is read-only: visitors browse the world, the heroes, the items, and watch what a structured game-content system looks like in Revisium.
